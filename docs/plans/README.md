# Plans

Keep active plans here as `YYYY-MM-DD Descriptive title.md`. Move finished plans
to `completed/` with their checklists and validation results intact. This
follows Pixui's repository-local planning convention; its product plans are not
copied.

Plans should include:

- Goal, scope, and explicit exclusions.
- Current foundation and constraints.
- Proposed design, ownership, failure handling, and alternatives.
- An actionable implementation checklist.
- Verification of observable behavior and relevant boundaries.
- Assumptions and open questions.

Resolve architecture questions before building around them. Record significant
choices separately in `docs/decisions/` and link them from the plan. Update
checklists during implementation; mark a plan complete only after its required
verification has passed.
